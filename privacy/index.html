Still

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Still is a Bible reading, devotional and prayer journal app for iPhone, published by HumfDev (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who processes it, and how you can see, export or delete it.

In short: you can read the whole Bible without an account or a connection. What you write in the app is private to your account. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device only

  • The Bible text (World English Bible), which is part of the app.
  • Reading preferences: text size, appearance, and the last passage you read.
  • A copy of the name, study goal, tradition and reminder settings you entered.
  • A copy of your current devotional plan, unfinished reflection and prayer drafts and which days you completed, so Today works offline.
  • Reminder times and the identifiers of reminders scheduled on this device.
  • Whether you have allowed the AI study assistant.

Bible search runs on your device. What you search for in the Bible reader is not sent to us.

Information stored in your account

The first time the app is opened with an internet connection it creates an anonymous guest account, so your prayers and progress have somewhere private to be saved. No name, email address or password is required. If you delete your account and keep using the app, a new empty guest account is created. The following is stored in your account on our servers:

  • Account identifier: a random user ID. If you choose Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple issues for this app is linked to it. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • Profile and settings: the name you ask to be greeted by (optional), your study goal (optional), the Christian tradition you select (optional), minutes per day, your time zone, reminder preferences and your privacy choices.
  • Prayers: each prayer’s title, description, the person it is for (optional), status, reminder time and any updates you add.
  • Reflections: journal text you choose to save, including AI answers you save as a reflection, and the passages it relates to.
  • Devotional plans and progress: the plans created for you and the days you completed.
  • Bookmarks: passages you save.
  • Question history: only if you turn on “Keep question history”, the questions you ask the study assistant and its answers. It is off by default.
  • Usage counters: how many AI requests your account made in a day or week, and the size of those requests, used to enforce fair-use limits. These counters contain no question, prayer or journal text.

Prayers, the tradition you select and what you write may reveal your religious beliefs. We store this information only because you enter it, only to provide the app’s features to you, and you can delete it at any time.

The AI study assistant and Google

Still uses Google’s Gemini models, through Google Cloud Vertex AI, to write answers and suggestions. The app asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent, and you can withdraw it at Settings > Privacy and AI > AI study assistant. Bible reading, search, bookmarks, the prayer journal and the ready-made reading plans work without it.

When you use an AI feature, the following is sent through our server to Google to produce the result:

  • Ask: your question, the most recent questions and answers in the same conversation, the Bible passages our server selected, and the Christian tradition you chose, if any.
  • Personalized plan: the study goal you type, the minutes per day and the tradition you chose, if any.
  • Organize a prayer: the prayer note you ask to have organized, with today’s date so a follow-up date can be suggested. This happens only when you tap the button for it.

Your saved prayers, reflections and bookmarks are not sent to the AI. Your user ID, name and Apple identifier are not included in what is sent. Google processes this text on our behalf under its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Google states that it does not use it to train its AI models. Google may hold it briefly, cached for up to 24 hours and logged to detect abuse of its service, as described in Google’s data retention documentation.

Please do not include information about other people, or anything you would not want processed this way, in a question.

Technical information

Like any internet service, the servers that run Still (Google Firebase and Google Cloud) receive your IP address and basic device and app information when the app connects, and keep short-lived security and diagnostic logs. Our own server logs record which operation ran, whether it succeeded, how long it took and how large it was. They do not record the text of questions, prayers or reflections.

Still contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. Notifications are scheduled on your device; reminder notifications never show the content of a prayer.

How we use information

  • To provide the features you use: keeping your prayers, reflections, plans and bookmarks, and producing AI answers when you ask for them.
  • To keep the service secure and within fair-use limits.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, use it to build advertising profiles, or use it to train AI models. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (for the religious information you choose to enter and for sending text to the AI) and on providing the service you asked for. You can withdraw consent by deleting the content or your account, or by turning the AI study assistant off.

Who processes your information

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Vertex AI) hosts your account data and runs the AI models, as our service provider. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple, to confirm your identity.

We do not share your information with anyone else, unless we are required to by law or it is necessary to protect someone’s safety.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept until you delete it. You are in control:

  • Delete everything: Settings > Your data > Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your account, profile, prayers and their updates, reflections, plans, progress, bookmarks, question history and usage counters from our servers, and clears the app’s data on your device. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to revoke its sign-in token. Residual copies in Google’s backups and logs expire on Google’s normal schedule.
  • Delete individual items: Settings > Privacy and AI > View and delete saved data, or delete a prayer from the prayer itself.
  • Export a copy: Settings > Your data > Export my data produces a file with everything stored in your account.
  • Stop saving questions: turn off “Keep question history”. Questions are then answered without being saved.

A guest account exists only on the device that created it. If you delete the app without linking an Apple ID, we cannot identify or recover that account. If you can no longer open the app and want its data removed, use the Still support page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. The export and delete controls above are available to everyone. For any other request, use the Still support page.

Children

Still is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each account’s data can be read and changed only by that account, and AI credentials are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not store information in the app that would put you or someone else at risk if it were disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this policy and its date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: use the Still support page.
