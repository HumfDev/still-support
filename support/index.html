Help with Still, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Send a support request. We aim to reply within a few days.

Support requests sent this way are publicly visible. Please do not include private details or the text of your prayers.

Delete your account and data

Open the app, tap the settings icon on the Today tab, then Settings > Your data > Delete account and data, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored in it, and clears the app’s data on your device. If you signed in with Apple you will be asked to confirm with Apple first.

If you can no longer open the app, contact us using the details above. A guest account that was never linked to an Apple ID cannot be identified once the app has been deleted from the device.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. The Bible, search and a ready-made reading plan work straight away. A private guest account, with no name or email, is created automatically the first time the app is online.

Will I lose my data if I get a new phone? A guest account lives only on one device. Link an Apple ID at Settings > Account before you switch, then sign in with the same Apple ID on the new device.

Does it work offline? Bible reading, search and your current devotional plan work offline. Prayers, saved reflections and the AI study assistant need a connection.

Why did the study assistant say I reached a limit? AI features have daily fair-use limits. The message shows when the limit resets.

An AI answer was wrong or inappropriate. What should I do? Tap “Report this answer” under the answer, or contact us. Answers are generated by an AI model and can be mistaken; always compare them with the passage itself.

How do I turn the AI off? Settings > Privacy and AI > AI study assistant. Nothing is sent to the AI while it is off.

My reminder did not arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Still in the iOS Settings app, and that a Focus mode is not silencing it.

Which Bible translation is this? The World English Bible, Protestant edition, a public-domain translation published by eBible.org.

If you need help right now

Still is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. In the United States and Canada you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
